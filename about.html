Wesley & Kyle.

A locally owned father and son company doing residential, commercial and industrial work across the region.

Started with the bare essentials.

Wesley Hack started the company in 2000 with the bare essentials, and it has grown ever since. His son Kyle took on work in the company as early as he could, and today the two of them run it together.

The work started with homes and grew into commercial and industrial sites: sod and trees for distribution centers, walls for restaurants, basin plantings for new developments, and snow and ice management through the winter.

In 2020 Wesley switched every trimmer, blower and mower over to battery powered Greenworks equipment, then built a solar charging station at the shop to keep it all running. In August 2021 that put him on the cover of Lawn & Landscape magazine.
