01 / What we do

Year round, any size.

"No project is too big or too small." Homes around Millville, storefronts and restaurants, and warehouse sites across Pennsylvania and into New York and Virginia.

  • 01

    Lawn maintenance

    Mowing, string trimming, edging, hedge trimming, weed control, aerating and fertilization. Weekly or bi-weekly service available.

  • 02

    Spring & fall cleanup

    Leaf cleanup, mulching, shrub and hedge trimming, weed control and debris removal, on an as needed basis.

  • 03

    Hardscaping

    Retaining walls, patios, sidewalks and pathways, including Redi-Rock block wall systems.

  • 04

    Design & installation

    Landscape design, planting beds, sod, tree and shrub installation, decorative stone and mulch.

  • 05

    Commercial & industrial Site work

    Sod by the tens of thousands of square feet, seeding and erosion matting, basin plantings and trees for distribution centers and retail sites.

  • 06

    Snow & ice Winter

    Plowing, blowing, removal, relocation, pretreating and salting for commercial and industrial lots.
